MemoChat E-Hentai Authentication

Idle - waiting for MemoChat
This extension helps MemoChat securely access your E-Hentai account without storing your password.

How to use:

  1. Open MemoChat web application (http://localhost:* or http://127.0.0.1:*)
  2. Navigate to R18 Sources → E-Hentai account settings
  3. Click "网页登录" (Web Login)
  4. This extension will open the official E-Hentai forum login page
  5. Enter your credentials on the official page (your password never leaves E-Hentai)
  6. After login, the extension captures your session cookies and sends them to MemoChat
